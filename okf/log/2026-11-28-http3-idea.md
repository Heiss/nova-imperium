The owner asked whether HTTP/3 would make loading and the data exchange faster (2026-11-28). HTTP/3 is only the first hop from the browser; in the owner's chain (HAProxy in `mode tcp` → WireGuard → NPMplus, where TLS ends → Caddy → Node) it needs UDP 443 past HAProxy through the tunnel to NPMplus, and an NPMplus built with HTTP/3 [U]. Decided: an idea in the backlog, after #409 and #482, measured before and after (#489); the chain goes neither into the operator texts nor into the CI spec of O29; HTTP/2 stays the requirement ([Data exchange and realtime](/architecture/realtime.md)).
