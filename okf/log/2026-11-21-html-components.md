#337, the follow-up of #330 (owner and coordinator [V], 2026-11-21): the `{@html}` that showed a value of a player or of the game became Svelte markup and components, view by view and with no visible change. `Cost.svelte` and `Requirements.svelte` replace `costHTML()` and `requirementsHTML()` in `CatalogList`, `LevelTable` and `LexicanumArticle`; `PlanetPicture.svelte` and `MoonPicture.svelte` replace `planetHTML()` and `moonPicture()` in the overview and the Sternenkarte; in the Sternenkarte the symbols of a moon and a Trümmerfeld are data (`GalaxyMark` of `logic/galaxy.ts`) and the button „Bergung“ is the action kind `harvest`, both drawn by `Galaxy.svelte` (the `html` kind is gone); `MarketPreview.svelte` replaces `previewHTML()` of the marketplace. The builders that lost their last caller are deleted (`costHTML`, `requirementsHTML`, `planetHTML`, `moonPicture`, `moonMark`, `debrisMark`, `harvestButton`, `previewHTML`); knip checks it. `Fleet.svelte` had no `{@html}` left, #332 removed them.

Proof of no change: the frozen copies of the deleted builders are `src/test/html-pieces-frozen.ts`; the existing reference tests of the lists, the overview, the Sternenkarte and the marketplace stay green unchanged (the first three import the builders they used to take from the live code from the frozen file, the only edit of those tests; the marketplace test froze its preview itself). New: `src/test/html-pieces-reference.test.ts` compares the four small components with the frozen builders on generated cases (seed 337; names with `<`, `&` and quotes stay text), and `src/test/cost-views-reference.test.ts` compares every cost line of the level table and of every article of the Lexicanum with `costHTML()`. The unit tests of the deleted builders moved to the components (`views/galaxy.test.ts`, `views/market.test.ts`) or now assert the data (`logic/galaxy.test.ts`).

What stays with `{@html}`: the fixed SVG glyphs `TAB_GLYPHS` of `TabBar.svelte`, `MoreList.svelte` and `Bell.svelte`, which hold no value of a player or of the game (decided), and the profile of a guild in `Guilds.svelte`. The profile is player text: the server renders the description with markdown-it (`html: false`, images off) into `profileHtml`, so the page shows markup that a player wrote as Markdown (headings, lists, links). Changing that is a decision, not a conversion, so it was not touched; the owner decided the same day (Q41) that it stays as the one exception with data of a player, noted next to the code, and that no conversion is planned. The order of the planet list is now the named comparator `byPlace` of `logic/moon.ts`, whose contract (a moon after its planet, whichever is compared first) is tested directly: the removed builders had taken their killed mutants with them, and the three survivors left in the inline comparator showed that only one direction of it was tested.
