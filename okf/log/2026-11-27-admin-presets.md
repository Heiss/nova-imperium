The admin gets presets of the round settings (#441, owner, 2026-11-27, 234, 237a): the row „Regelset“ in the dialog „Neue Runde“ and in the tab „Universum“ has the preset „Standard“, today's defaults. A click asks first; „Umstellen“ fills the fields, under „Universum“ only the settings that change in a running round, and saves nothing; only the save applies them. Every setting can carry a mark „klassisch“ or „neu“ (235b); the marks follow once the owner confirms the list (see [Admin](/features/admin.md)).
