Follow-up of #357 by the owner's decision of 2026-11-22: the fourteen headings of the settings are approved, and a legend of the dialog „Neue Runde“ that repeats the heading of its group is no longer shown. The fieldsets „Aktivität“, „Fremde Flotten erkennen“, „Expeditionen“, „Marktplatz“ and „Planetengrenze“ keep their frame and are named by the heading of their group (`aria-labelledby`); „Aktivität“ was not among the four the owner named but repeats its heading, so the same rule removes it [V]. „Belohnungen für die neue Runde“ and „Rückbau“ differ from their headings and stay. The tests that read those legends now find the fieldset by its legend or by the heading that names it (`fieldsetOpen` and `outsideFieldsets` of `src/test/markup.ts`), and assert that the repeated legend is gone; the reference test of the dialog puts the heading back as the legend before it compares with the markup before #118.
