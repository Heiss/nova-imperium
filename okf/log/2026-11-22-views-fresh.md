Fix of #361 (owner, 2026-11-22: all of them for 26.11.25): the fleet, the marketplace, „Freunde“, „Achievements“, „Gilden“ and „Admin“ were mounted anew on every render, so a render that is not fresh replaced their fields while the player typed, as on the Sternenkarte (#352) and in „Optionen“ (#355, #359). Reproduced for the fleet on `main` first: with a render between the select and the insert of a `fill()` of „Galaxie“ or „System“, the field held 22 or 11 and „Auftrag“ fell to „Landnahme“ with only „Landnahme“ and „Vermessung“ offered, the failure of CI run 45005599359 (`e2e/fleet-quick-targets.spec.ts:169`); with the position it held 44 and „Auftrag“ was „Expedition“. The six views now say `fresh` and share one mount, `keptMount()` of `src/client/views/kept.ts` (`KeptView.svelte`): a fresh render still mounts them anew, any other render hands them the new page. A countdown (`Due.svelte`) gets a new element for a new end, so a kept view never carries the clock's mark of a fired countdown into the next one. A guard in `src/client/views/registry.test.ts` reads the components of every view from the sources and refuses a view with a typed field that is `anew`; its list of exceptions is empty, as #359 converted „Optionen“.

Tests: `e2e/views-fresh.spec.ts` (each of the six views: a typed field selected, a poll render, then typed over: exactly what was typed; all failed before), `e2e/views-kept.spec.ts` (the focus in every view, the countdown of the fleet and the place of the guild chat over a poll render; passed before and after) and the guard. Changed existing tests, each only in how it learns of the poll render (every assertion stays, no timeout changed; each failed with a temporary change that dropped every draft on every render): in `e2e/redraw-drafts.spec.ts` the cases of the fleet form, the marketplace, the new guild, the filter of the achievements, the opened details of the event log and of the error log, and the filters of the event log and of the error log (old: the replacement of a marked node of the view, `waitForRedraw`/`waitForReplaced`, now removed; new: `pollRender` of `e2e/helpers.ts`, a new colony in the header's choice of the planet, drawn by the same `render()` pass as the view); in `e2e/fleet-form.spec.ts` the cases „Vermessung“ and „Mond“ (the same old and new signal); in `e2e/round-archive.spec.ts` the refresh of the preview (old: the replacement of the button „Vorschau aktualisieren“; new: the new player in the player choice of the event log). The unit tests of the mounting of the marketplace and of „Freunde“ say `fresh` now.
