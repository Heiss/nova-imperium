#118, after bundle 7 [V]: the panels of „Admin“ that fill themselves are Svelte components. `AdminDiagnostics.svelte` (the filter of the event log, the event log, the player records with „Mond anlegen“ and the moderation, the orders), `AdminErrors.svelte` (the list of the errors with its filter, the paging and the download range) and `AdminInvitations.svelte` (the list with „Widerrufen“) render the pure functions of the new `logic/admin-panels.ts` (tables, pager, details, the days of a filter) through `AdminTable.svelte` and `AdminPager.svelte`. What they loaded, what was typed into the filters and the tab that is shown are the state of `logic/admin-panels.svelte.ts`, which also holds the calls (the newest request of a list shows its result, as before), the opening of a tab and the arrow keys of the tab list, so `Admin.svelte` shows `aria-selected`, `tabindex`, `hidden` and `aria-labelledby` itself and the DOM modules `views/admin-panel.ts`, `admin-diagnostics.ts` and `admin-errors.ts` are gone. The saving of the ZIP of the errors is `client/download.ts`; `features/admin.ts` binds the calls of the frame. The page keeps the panels for the same admin over a render and starts new ones for another admin. Same German texts, columns, buttons, ids and `data-` marks (the e2e tests are unchanged). Reference tests (seed 118, `normalize()` and `textContents()`) compare the panels with their frozen HTML (`src/test/admin-panels-reference.test.ts`); the frame test of „Admin“ now includes the panels and the painted tab. `main.ts` changed by one import line (`forgetAdminPanel`); 888 lines as before. No server change, no visible change; see [Design patterns](/architecture/patterns.md), [Error log](/features/error-log.md).
