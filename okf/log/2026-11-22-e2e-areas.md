The E2E run of #347 failed once in `e2e/options-steady.spec.ts`: the home planet of the test could not move into its area, „[2:61:4] is taken by another planet“. Cause: a new round puts the home planet of every player on a random position of any galaxy (`src/server/game/admin.ts`), also into an area no test had been handed yet, while the areas of #302 assumed that only galaxy 1 gets random homes. Reproduced with 120 players and one new round: 16 of the next 200 areas held planets. The rule of the game stays; `ownArea` of `e2e/own-data.ts` now hands out the first area without a planet (`firstEmptyArea` of `e2e/ledger.ts`, the planets read by `e2e/area-planets.ts`) and records each area it passes over in the ledger.
