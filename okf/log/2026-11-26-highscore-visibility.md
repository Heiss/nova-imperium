The admin can let the highscore show every category, the total only, or nothing (#422, owner, 2026-11-26), in the round settings under „Highscore zeigt“, effective at once. With the total only, Wirtschaft and Militär are not sent. With nothing, the game sends no points or ranks of other players: the Highscore page and its menu entry are hidden, the Übersicht shows the own points without a place, and the Sternenkarte and the guilds show no ranks or points. The „(n)“ of the noob protection stays, the round archive keeps showing finished rounds in full, and the final ranking is computed as before.
