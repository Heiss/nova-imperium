The owner's three additions of 2026-11-19 to „Wache stellen“ (#55, comment „drei Ergänzungen“) built on `claude/hold-decisions`, [E] in [Holding and ACS](/features/holding-acs.md#the-three-decisions-of-2026-11-19-55) and [Vacation mode](/features/vacation-mode.md) (#223). 1. Held fleets defend in the Sturmangriff, the Mondbruch (#95) and the fight of detected Auspex probes (#175): each mission asks `defenders` of `src/server/game/fight.ts` and the one shared battle gives every held fleet its own party, its losses, its debris and the report of the defender for each of its owners; missiles do not fight. 2. When the host starts the vacation mode, the fleets that hold at his planets and moons fly home at once (`sendHome`, the flight back as long as the flight there); a Wache stellen still on its way, which `startVacation` leaves flying as launched flights always did, does not start to hold at its arrival but turns home, so the earlier „arrives, holds and flies home“ is gone. Whoever has a fleet of his own out still cannot start the vacation. 3. `fleets.reroute` takes the fleets in state `hold` too: a fleet that holds at a broken moon moves to its planet, keeps its ships, cargo and end time and holds on; one at the planet is untouched. No migration. [V] A detected spying at a planet with neither ships nor defense but with a held fleet fights that fleet. [V] No new German text: the owner of a fleet sent home or moved gets no notice of its own, only the existing return report. Open points for the owner, each with options. A. The limits of the planet (15 fleets, four players) after a move: 1. the fleet moves anyway (built [V]); 2. the fleets above the limit fly home at once, the latest first; 3. every fleet that moved flies home at once. B. The Mondbruch rolls only after a won battle, and a won battle leaves no defender, so a foreign fleet that holds at the moon fights and is destroyed whenever the moon can break; decision 3 reaches only the attacker's own fleet that holds there, who stays out of the battle: 1. keep it as built; 2. held fleets do not fight in the Mondbruch, so they move with the broken moon (takes decision 1 back for the Mondbruch); 3. they fight, but the roll after the battle counts a battle won against the moon's own ships and defense only, and the held fleets that survive move to the planet (changes the rule „only after a won battle“ of the Mondbruch). C. A notice for the owner of a fleet sent home by a vacation or moved by the Mondbruch: 1. none (built); 2. an info report at the moment, for example „Deine Wache bei [g:s:p] wurde heimgeschickt: Der Gastgeber hat den Urlaubsmodus begonnen.“ and „Dein Mond … wurde zerstört; deine Wache hält jetzt am Planeten.“; 3. only a push. D. The Promethium of the unused holding hours after a vacation is not refunded (as after a recall): 1. as built; 2. refund the share of the unused hours.
