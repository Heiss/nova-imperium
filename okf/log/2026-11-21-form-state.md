#332, a follow-up of package 8 of #118: forms keep their fields as the state of their component instead of the uniform field drafts. A form's state is a module of its own, as a view that is mounted anew loses what its component held (`logic/form-state.svelte.ts`, dropped by a fresh render and a logout); the component shows it and the handlers read and write it. Converted: the filter of the achievements and the position of the Sternenkarte (before this entry), the marketplace, the fleet form for the ships, the target position, the speed, the cargo and the names to invite into a union, „Optionen“ (choices, passwords, the text of „Problem melden“), „Gilden“ (profile, rank names, a new rank, the rank of a member, the rights, a disabled box showing the server as in #163) and „Admin“ (invitation, message to all players; the filters already kept their own state). A number field takes `value` with `oninput`, not `bind:value`: checked against Svelte's `bind_value`, a binding stores a number or null and, with a getter that has a fallback, writes the fallback over a half-typed value. One visible fix: after a sent report the poll redraw no longer puts the sent text back into „Problem melden“. It stays, with a changelog fragment ([V], coordinator 2026-11-21).

Part two of the fleet form, and the end of the drafts (#332, the same day, [V] of the coordinator under the rule of AGENTS.md, consistent with the owner's decision). The kind of target, the mission, the union, the holding time and the stay, and the „Zielmond“ of the Warp-Tor are state of `logic/fleet-form.svelte.ts` too, and the list „Auftrag“, the note of the limit of planets, „Flotte losschicken“, the row „Haltezeit“ and the moon option are derived in `Fleet.svelte` from that state by one pure function, `fleetChoice()` of `logic/fleet-choice.ts` (the handlers of `features/fleet.ts` and `features/union.ts` read the same function, so the form never shows one thing and sends another). The HTML strings that `missionList` and `timeRow` rewrote after every render, the `{@html}` blocks and the writes into the fields are gone; the „Schnellwahl“ has no state of its own, it enters the target into the state and goes back to „– wählen –“. No visible change: the frozen reference test of the fleet view (`src/test/fleet-reference.test.ts`) is unchanged and green. One detail kept as it was: the mission, the kind of target and the union chosen are remembered for the next form of a fresh render in `client.ui` (`fm`, `fk`, `union`), and the row of the time that is not shown starts again with 1 hour.

The amounts of the four lists (`q_*`, `td_*`) do not depend on the drafts: the poll redraws the lists in place, and their two fields carry no value, binding or handler of the component. `src/client/views/list-fields.test.ts` checks both facts (the lists are not mounted anew; the syntax tree of `CatalogList.svelte`), and `e2e/redraw-drafts.spec.ts` types into `q_abm` and `td_abm`, lets the poll draw the view and reads them again. With that, `field-drafts.ts`, `logic/field-drafts.ts` (with its tests), `restoreFields`, `forgetFields`, `trackFields` and `keeps` of `logic/jump-gate.ts` are deleted, and the guard `src/test/field-writes.test.ts` has no allow-list entry any more: it fails on every direct write to a field except inside `setField` of `src/client/dom.ts`. The inventory of where each field keeps its value is in `okf/architecture/patterns/13-client-features.md` („Per-form component state“).
