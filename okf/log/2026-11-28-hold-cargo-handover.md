The cargo of a destroyed hold goes to the host's planet (owner, 2026-11-28, #467, replacing 233b). Right after a battle in which holds defend, a hold keeps what still fits into its survivors (the order of the loot) and hands the rest to the planet as a transport delivers, so a plunder that follows takes half of it with the stock. In every battle with holds: Sturmangriff, Mondbruch and the fight of detected Auspex probes, whoever wins. The defender's battle report shows the handed cargo per hold [V]. The Lexicanum sentence of the pages „Verbündete“ and „Kampf“ and the changelog entry say the new rule [V].
