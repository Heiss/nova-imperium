The owner's rule of 2026-11-27 (259b, 260a, 261a, 262b, 263a) is now `okf/ops/feature-switches.md`. Every feature gets an on/off switch per round, classic and comfort features included, so a round can be played with the classic rules alone or with new features. The core of the scope in the overview stays always on, with sign-in, account and admin. A new switch is „An“ in running universes, and in „Neue Runde“ the preset of #441 applies. Existing features without a switch get one through a collection issue. The proposals cover how a switch is shown and audited, what is hidden while it is off (as #404), and that every feature issue states what switching it off during a round does (examples: #440 and #386).
