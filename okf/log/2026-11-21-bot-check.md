#181 (owner, 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-21, [E]; coordinator Q37 to Q40, [V]): the admin can switch „Automatisierung prüfen“ on for one player in the player record ([Check for automated play](/features/bot-check.md)). Off by default, on until the admin turns it off; the switch is an admin write in the audit log. While it is on, the record lists per day since the switch-on raw facts the server keeps anyway: the game actions, those without active play time, the hits of the action limit (#328) and the gaps between the actions (shortest, median, middle half), at most 30 days back, though the play time (#231) is kept longer; no score, no threshold, no consequence, no signals of the browser (#232). Nothing is stored for the hints, nothing reaches the player, and everything goes with the account. The Lexicanum has a new rule page „Fair spielen“; changelog fragment `changes/fair-spielen.md`.
