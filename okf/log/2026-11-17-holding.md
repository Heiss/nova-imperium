#55 built (part of #222) with the owner's answers of 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-17: the mission Halten, shown as „Wache stellen“ (owner, 2026-11-17), flies to the planet of an ally with a holding time of 0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 hours, pays max(⌊Σ ships × base consumption × hours / 10⌋, 1) Promethium for it at the launch (D2), holds there and flies home; at most four players and 15 fleets hold at one planet. The battle takes several parties (`battleOf`, bit for bit as before with two), the shared contract #225 builds on: every fleet that holds defends with the owner, each owner gets the defender's report, the debris takes every party, and a raid loots the classic half of the planet's stock first, then the held cargo with the room left (owner, 2026-11-17). Who may hold is the admin's value per round „Halten erlaubt für“ (guild, friends or both, default both, Q2), set in „Neue Runde“ and live in the universe settings (X1); friends are nobody until the buddy list #227, and guild members count only while guilds are on (#191). The alliance depot stays later (D1); the vacation mode #223 protects the target of every mission in its own check. Migration `20261117T1029-holding` rebuilds the fleets with the mission `hold`, the state `hold` and `fleets.hold`; the mission follows the strategies of #116 with the new target kind `ally` and the typed field `holds`. After the build the owner decided (2026-11-17): the name „Wache stellen“, a second value per round „Gehaltene Flotten im Auspex-Bericht ab Auspex-Lehre“ (default 2: below it the held fleets count into the planet's fleet in a spy report, from it they show apart), and the Auspex-Phalanx shows a held fleet as holding until the end of its holding time. The [V] choices are listed in [Holding and ACS](/features/holding-acs.md).
