Fix of #355 (the owner on his phone: „Optionen“ jumps back and forth while scrolling and while setting the notifications, so a tap hits the wrong switch). Measured on the phone project (Pixel 7): „Optionen“ was mounted anew on every render, so a render that is not fresh (the poll after a change of the state, the news, the two renders of the save of a switch) emptied the page for a moment (1655 px fell to 903 px, the browser clamped the scroll position) and the focus given back by id scrolled the tapped switch into view; the page moved by 50 px on an untouched render and by 118 to 316 px per tap, and a tap aimed at „Forschung abgeschlossen“ 400 ms after another tap switched „Auspex-Aktivität bei deinen Planeten“. The view now says `fresh`, as the Sternenkarte does since #352: a fresh render still mounts it anew, any other render hands the mounted component the new page (`OptionsView.svelte`), so the page keeps its elements. What the features fill after a render stays stable: `#own-activity` is painted again into the same element, `#pwa-install` keeps its element and only its `hidden` changes, and `#passkey-list` (filled by its button only) now stays until a fresh render instead of being emptied by every render. Tests: `e2e/options-steady.spec.ts` on the desktop and the phone (scrolling every section of the phone switch while the news and the poll draw, and three switches tapped with a poll after them: no move of the scroll position or of the aimed switch, and the aimed tap hits its switch; three of the four cases failed before, the desktop scroll case moved nothing before either) and the unit test of the mounting in `src/client/views/options.test.ts`. One existing test changed, as the coordinator allowed for the Sternenkarte (#352): `e2e/error-log.spec.ts`, the case „a player reports a problem in „Hilfe & Optionen““, told the poll render by the replacement of the field `#reportform-text`, which the fix stops; it now seeds a colony and waits until the list of „Hauptplanet“ in „Optionen“ shows it. Its assertion is unchanged, and it fails when the sent text is put back by a render that is not fresh (checked with a temporary change). The new spec starts to measure once the page's own first loads have answered (the round archive and `#own-activity`): a one-time load of the page is not a render, and where it grows a box above the viewport the browser's scroll anchoring changes the scroll position to keep the content in place (on the desktop the box of the play time grew from 23 to 609 px above the viewport; scroll position 894 to 1496, the visible content did not move). That one-time growth is a separate matter for the coordinator.
