The (n) of the Sternenkarte follows the exact points of the server, as an attack does (#405, owner, 2026-11-26). While the map is shown, the browser names the shown system with its requests; the state answers which owners there the noob protection covers. Before that list is there no (n) is shown, and a switch to another system loads the state. Measured on 4 cores, median of 40 calls, 3 planets per player, 15 owners in the shown system: 20 players 4.0–5.0 ms without the map, 7.0–9.9 ms with it; 40 players 6.5 ms without, 10.6–13.2 ms with it; a system without other owners costs nothing extra.
