Fix of #362 (live on 26.11.24: „Flotte“ threw `each_key_duplicate` for one player, 26 errors and 3 rejections in the error log). Reproduced first: a player who sends a probe to the main planet of a friend, or of a guild member, had that place twice in the „Schnellwahl“ (as „Freund“ or „Gilde“ and again as „Ziel“), the list is keyed by the id of the place, and right after the launch the page showed „https://svelte.dev/e/each_key_duplicate“ instead of „Flotte unterwegs nach …“ (`e2e/quick-duplicates.spec.ts`, desktop and phone, failed 4 of 4 before). The place now stands once, under the first group that has it: friend, then guild, then last target (the owner's rule of 2026-11-17 for a friend who is a guild member, extended to the last targets [V]). The server leaves such a last target out and still fills up to five with older ones (`quickState`); the browser keeps the first entry of an id whatever the server sends (`quickTargets`). The other keyed lists of the fleet page were checked: the unions and the open attacks (ids from the server, the unions from a set), the ships (catalog keys), the missions (a filter of `FLEET_MISSIONS`), the unions to join (union ids), the jump targets (the own gates), the fleets in flight (the server lists each fleet once in one filter, `stateFor`), the hours, the speeds and the cargo fields (constants), the planets of the header and the cells of the resource bar; tests in `src/client/logic/fleet-keys.test.ts` and `src/server/game/state.test.ts`. The existing case „is the own places, the friends, the guild members and the last targets in this order, in one list“ of `src/client/logic/fleet-quick.test.ts` expected the place of a guild member twice; it now expects it once.
