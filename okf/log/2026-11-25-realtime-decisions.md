The owner's decisions on the data exchange between server and browser (2026-11-25), in the new concept [Data exchange and realtime](/architecture/realtime.md): stage 3, that is Server-Sent Events that say something changed, fetches with an ETag, the 4 s poll as a slow fallback and updates in place in the client; HTTP/2 to the browser as a requirement; no buffering of the event route by any proxy, the app sends `X-Accel-Buffering: no`; streams only with a valid session, a maximum per account and in total, a heartbeat; volumetric attacks are the operator's; operator texts for nginx, Apache and Caddy with the pull request of the events, checked by E2E tests through real proxies in the CI. Cobalt27 is rejected: nothing is gained over the standards. The data travel as parts of the state, each with its own version and ETag, fetched in full once and then only the parts an event names; deltas and GraphQL per component not chosen (O26, owner, the same day). ETags for reading the parts, no `If-Match` on game actions, so a change elsewhere never refuses a click (owner, the same day). Checks against a stale view (O31, the same day): forms send `basedOn` and only the changed fields, a stamp per field refuses only a field changed meanwhile (universe settings, guild profile, name and rights of a rank); the marketplace carries the price seen and refuses any worse one; the „alles“ buttons send the intent. A guild log as a feature of its own (O32, [Guild log](/features/guild-log.md)). Open with options: the limits (O27), several tabs (O28), the proxies in the CI (O29), and `main` or v3 (O30) ([Open points](/open-points.md)).
