The owner's decisions on the data exchange between server and browser (2026-11-25), in the new concept [Data exchange and realtime](/architecture/realtime.md): stage 3, that is Server-Sent Events that say something changed, fetches with an ETag, the 4 s poll as a slow fallback and updates in place in the client; HTTP/2 to the browser as a requirement; no buffering of the event route by any proxy, the app sends `X-Accel-Buffering: no`; streams only with a valid session, a maximum per account and in total, a heartbeat; volumetric attacks are the operator's; operator texts for nginx, Apache and Caddy with the pull request of the events, checked by E2E tests through real proxies in the CI. Cobalt27 is rejected: nothing is gained over the standards. The data travel as parts of the state, each with its own version and ETag, fetched in full once and then only the parts an event names; deltas and GraphQL per component not chosen (O26, owner, the same day). ETags for reading the parts, no `If-Match` on game actions, so a change elsewhere never refuses a click (owner, the same day). The requirement behind it: the game runs on weak hardware shared with other services, such as a Celeron J4104 ([Overview](/overview.md)); a measurement found the time of the server, not the bytes, as the cost, three quarters of it the points and highscore of every player on every call. So first these are computed in one pass at most every 10 s, since points also change with time without a write (live statistics since 0.78c, owiki), and the parts the same for every player (`galaxy`, `players`, universe, round) are built, serialized and compressed once per version. Checks against a stale view (O31, the same day): forms send `basedOn`; a save holds all or nothing; any change of the part by someone else since `basedOn` refuses the whole save until „Übernehmen“, the safest choice first; a stamp per field names who changed what (universe settings, guild profile, each rank); the marketplace carries the price seen and refuses any worse one; the „alles“ buttons send the intent. A guild log as a feature of its own (O32, [Guild log](/features/guild-log.md)). The total of streams and the heartbeat (30 s) are environment variables, at least 2 streams per account or a notice in the admin panel at the invitations; the reconnect follows the server's `retry:`; the page shows when it has no connection; several tabs: a hidden tab closes its stream, the oldest goes above the limit (O28); the CI runs a focused spec through nginx, Apache and Caddy (O29). The rest of the limits (O27): 5 streams per account, a total of 100 by default, `retry:` an environment variable defaulting to the heartbeat, limits per address measured in the E2E run, and clicks without a connection neither sent nor kept. The answer of an action carries the parts it changed with their ETags; the stamp is written in the repository method of its field, with a guard test, and a change no person made carries the actor „System“. The names `NOVA_EVENT_STREAMS`, `NOVA_EVENT_HEARTBEAT` and `NOVA_EVENT_RETRY`, the count of accounts and open invitations, and the two states of the connection shown on the page are confirmed. WebSocket, the sync engines and CRDTs are not pursued [E]. While the events are cut, the page polls every 15 s; the texts of #409 are confirmed. Per piece (O30): the cached points, the expected price and the intent of the „alles“ buttons to `main`; the events and parts, the forms of O31 and the guild log to v3 ([Open points](/open-points.md)).
