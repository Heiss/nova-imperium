The admin sets the cap of the moon chance (1 to 100 %, default 20) and the Adamant and Psikristall for 1 % of it (10,000 to 1,000,000 in steps of 10,000, default 100,000) per round, effective for the next battle (#433, owner, 2026-11-27). For the diameter the chance counts at most up to 20, so moons stay as big as before and the Mondbruch keeps working. „Mond anlegen“ takes 1 % up to the cap, and the Lexikon shows the set values (see [Moons](/features/moons.md)).
