The dialog of a message (#349, variant C of the design study). „Neue Nachricht“ and „Antworten“ below an open conversation open a native modal dialog titled like the button; the one-row form of #226 is gone. The focus goes to the first empty field, „Abbrechen“ and Escape return it to the button. Under „Empfänger“ the quick choice offers the conversations of the inbox, at most five, newest first („Zuletzt geschrieben:“), and every other friend („Freunde:“); no deleted account and no blocked player. The text field has eight lines and the count „n / 1000“; Enter makes a new line, Ctrl+Enter or Cmd+Enter sends. What was typed stays as a draft when the dialog closes without sending, one for „Neue Nachricht“ and one per conversation, until it is sent, the page is reloaded or the player logs out. The reply shows „An {Name}“ and the last message received under „Letzte Nachricht von {Name}“. A refusal of the server while the dialog is open shows inside the dialog, a line with `role="alert"` above the buttons in the server's words; it goes when the recipient or the text changes and when the dialog opens again, and the dialog keeps the draft. Every other refusal is a note as before. On a phone the dialog fills the screen; on the lock screen of the vacation mode „Antworten“ opens the same dialog. The server is unchanged; it still takes the line breaks out of a message (open point in the concept). Logic in `src/client/logic/pm-compose.ts`, components `PmDialog` and `PmCompose`, E2E `e2e/pm-compose.spec.ts`. Changed existing tests: the flows of `e2e/private-messages.spec.ts` and `e2e/vacation.spec.ts` open the dialog before they write, and the refusals of a message in `e2e/private-messages.spec.ts` are read from the dialog instead of the note.
