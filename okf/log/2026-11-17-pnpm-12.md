pnpm 12 (owner, 2026-11-17, #264): straight from 10.34.9 to 12.21.0; the settings move to `pnpm-workspace.yaml` (`allowBuilds`: `esbuild` built, `better-sqlite3` not, and `engineStrict`), because pnpm 11 and later no longer read the field `pnpm` of `package.json` or `.npmrc`; the Dockerfile copies the file; the security hotfix writes its overrides there for a release from pnpm 11 on and keeps `package.json` for an older one, and it replaces an older pin of the same package, which pnpm would apply first (CodeRabbit on #284), proven with both versions on a test project; pnpm's default release age of one day does not refuse an exactly pinned young version (tested) (see [System](/architecture/system.md), [Branching and release](/ops/branching-release.md)).
