#358 part 1 built with the owner's decisions of 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-27 (286c, 288a): a „?“ beside every setting of the tab „Universum“ and of the dialog „Neue Runde“ opens an admin-only side panel with an explanation for the admin and, where values follow from the setting, a table over its minimum, two values in between, its maximum and its current value. The texts live beside the registry entries (`explain`), are proposals [V] for the owner to check, and name no number; every cell is computed with the functions of `src/core`. The details and the open point are in [Admin](/features/admin.md#an-explanation-per-setting-behind-a--358-part-1).
