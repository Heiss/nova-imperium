The Handelsposten is not offered while the marketplace is off (#442, owner, 2026-11-27, 226b, 227b): it is not listed in the Lexicanum, under „Gebäude“ only where it has a level or one in the queue (with the refusal of the closed marketplace and „Zurückbauen“, 278a), and its next level is refused, by the server too. Built levels stay, count for the points and can be demolished with the refund; a level already in the queue finishes. With the marketplace on again, the building is back with its levels. The rule sits in one function, so the Börse (#386) only widens it (see [Marketplace](/features/marketplace.md)).
