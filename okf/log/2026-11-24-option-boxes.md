The boxes of „Optionen“ register themselves (#378; owner, 2026-11-24: the option boxes only; part of the foundation of v3, #351; a refactor without a change of behaviour). Each box is a module of `src/client/views/options/` that exports `optionBoxes` (key, order, section of the phone switch, its Svelte component, its part of the page and, for „Benachrichtigungen“, the condition that push kinds exist); the data and the condition are pure logic in `src/client/logic/options/<box>.ts`, so they stay under mutation testing (100 % on every file) as `logic/options.ts` was, and the module in `views/options/` only declares the box; `scripts/gen-features.ts` lists them in the git-ignored `boxes.gen.ts` (`OPTION_BOXES`), and a key or an order number twice stops the start, naming both files. `Options.svelte` keeps the switch of the sections and draws the boxes in their order; `optionsPage()` of `logic/options.ts` is the parts of all boxes together, so a new box edits neither. The sentences of „Account löschen“ moved to `logic/delete-account.ts` (`logic/options.ts` still exports them). The markup is the same up to the white space between the boxes and within a line of text. Tests: `src/client/views/options/boxes.test.ts` (every box has a key, an order number and a section; no key, order number or field of the page twice; the clash messages; `Options.svelte` and `logic/options.ts` name no box) and `src/test/gen-option-boxes.test.ts` (the generated list). Existing tests unchanged, except the census of the keyed lists in `src/client/views/keyed-lists.test.ts` (#369), data only: the loops of the choices, the main planet and the push kinds moved from `Options.svelte` to `ChoicesBox.svelte`, `MainPlanetBox.svelte` and `PushBox.svelte` with the same verdicts, and the new loop of the boxes is judged by the guard (coordinator, 2026-11-24, [V]).
