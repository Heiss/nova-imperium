The highscore shows Gesamt, Wirtschaft and Militär instead of Gebäude, Forschung, Flotte and Verteidigung (#422, owner, 2026-11-26). Wirtschaft counts building and research levels and Sonnenreliquiare, Militär ships, defense and missiles, each by number or by the resources spent; the admin sets which in the round settings, by number by default. Gesamt, the final ranking and the noob protection stay as they were. A new round archive keeps the three categories in the mode of the round's end; older archives keep their four.
